# Lili Kraus Discography

## Notes

[Recordings & Discography - Lili Kraus](https://lee.classite.com/music/Kraus/discography-kraus.htm) - [Archived copy](https://web.archive.org/web/20220813010215/https://lee.classite.com/music/Kraus/discography-kraus.htm)

Created April 6, 2003. Last updated July 7, 2015.

- This discography is imperfect because my source is much restricted. Many of her recordings (especially at 78s) are deleted from catalog. Any information will be welcomed. (Recording data not in this page, broadcast recording, or other rare CDs...)

- Japanese CD issues; EMI TOCE..., London POCL..., KI..., DG POCG... UCCG..., Archiv POCA..., Philips PHCP..., Sony 25DC..., SRCR..., SICC..., BMG BVCC..., Testament UCCN... Warner WPCC...

- Korean issues; DG RG..., Decca RD... Philips RP... (LP), JC KJCL... (LP) / KJCK... (Tape), Sony CCK... (CD)

- Columbia ML LP series are monaural releases

- Abbreviations

  - Nations; DE (Germany), FR (France), IT (Italy), AU (Australia), NZ (New Zealand), JP (Japan), KR (Korea)

  - AS; Electronically reprocessed stereo

  - Matrix No; 'CXE 9574, 9575, 9582 (-1, 1, 3)' means '9574-1, 9575-1, 9282-3'.

- Warner-Erato box issue (825646 242238, 31 CD set) is very important to this list, particularly for 78s info, of which photos can be seen [here](http://fischer.egloos.com/5575851) (my Korean blog).

- If possible, I excluded items from such labels as Dante/LYS, Iron Needle, Enterprise, etc. If you'd like to know the reason, see [Hall of Shame](http://www.musicandarts.com/shame.html).

- Special thanks to M. Arnaud Nader. His comments on Le Discophiles Français and Ducretet-Thomson recordings are very precious.

- Of course also thanks so much to the owner of [mikrokosmos.com](http://www.mikrokosmos.com/) who offered me the hosting space.
