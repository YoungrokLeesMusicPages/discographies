In this LP's Liner notes; "Mr. Casadesus and Mr. Francescatti have made this record so that its sale might benefit the work of the American Library in Paris... Only 2,760 copies of this record have been printed and the record-master destroyed; the artists will not record these works again." ps] I have this record at the present time (2010), of which backcover note says "On November 30th 1954 the 'Hommage à Chausson' by Robert Casadesus had its world premiere in New York at Carnegie Hall. Mr. Casadesus and Mr. Francescatti ahd been asked to give a benefit performance for the American Library in Paris. These artists together with the Guilet Quartet agreed, and decided to give this concert in honor of the French composer Ernest Chausson." Chausson's Concert was recorded on the next day of the concert, therefore I think the works might be recorded on the same day.
