# Walter Gieseking Discography

## Notes

- The original discography was published in two parts, covering J. S. Bach through Debussy and Dvořák through Trapp.
- The source describes the discography as imperfect because information about Pearl and other independent-label recordings was limited, and it welcomed additions concerning unlisted recording data, broadcasts, and rare CDs.
- Unless otherwise stated, recordings are monaural. Columbia 33CX releases are monaural.
- Japanese issue prefixes identified by the source include EMI TOCE, London POCL and KI, DG POCG and UCCG, Archiv POCA, Philips PHCP, Sony SRCR, BMG BVCC, Testament UCCN, and Warner WPCC.
- Source abbreviations include DE (Germany), FR (France), IT (Italy), AU (Australia), NZ (New Zealand), JP (Japan), KR (Korea), and AS (electronically reprocessed stereo).
- Album numbers were linked to jacket images when available. Releases from Dante/LYS, Iron Needle, Enterprise, and similar labels were generally excluded.
- The source thanks the owner of mikrokosmos.com for providing hosting space.
- Both parts were created August 20, 2001. Part 1 was last updated June 20, 2010; part 2 was last updated June 9, 2013.
