Created October 15, 2011. Last updated April 30, 2016.

- This discography is imperfect because my source is much restricted. I have little information on his recordings from Pearl, and other indepent labels. Any information will be welcomed. (Recording data not in this page, broadcast recording, or other rare CDs...)

- Unless mentioned, phonic is monaural.

- Japanese CD issues; EMI TOCE..., London POCL..., KI..., DG POCG... UCCG..., Archiv POCA..., Philips PHCP..., Sony SRCR..., BMG BVCC..., Testament UCCN... Warner WPCC...

- Decca/London LP; Decca LXT / LT, London LL / CM series is monaural.

- Korean issues; DG RG..., Decca RD... Philips RP... (LP), CBS JC KJCL... (LP) / KJCK... (Tape)

- Abbreviations

  - Nations; DE (Germany), FR (France), IT (Italy), AU (Australia), NZ (New Zealand), JP (Japan), KR (Korea)

  - AS; Electronically processed stereo

- Album numbers are linked to the jacket images in case I can get them.

- If possible, I excluded items from such labels as Dante/LYS, Iron Needle, Enterprise, etc. If you'd like to know the reason, see [Hall of Shame](http://www.musicandarts.com/shame.html).
