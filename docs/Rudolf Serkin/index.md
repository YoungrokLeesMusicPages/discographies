# Rudolf Serkin Discography

## Notes

[Discography & recordings - Rudolf Serkin](https://web.archive.org/web/20241203080050/https://lee.classite.com/music/Serkin/discography-serkin.htm)

Created November 10, 2000. Last updated April 13, 2011.

- This discography is imperfect because my source is much restricted. I don't have much information on his early EMI period, Sony monaural, DG Mozart recordings. Any information will be welcomed. (Recording data not in this page, broadcast recording, or other rare CDs...)

- Japanese Toshiba-EMI released 17-CD set 'The Art of Adolf Busch (TOCE-6781~97)'. This huge, precious, and very expensive set (about 400$) contains all of the HMV (EMI) recordings by A. Busch, and of course by R. Serkin as his accompanist, also.

- Japanese release; EMI TOCE-..., DG POCG... Philips PHCP... Telarc PHCT... Sony Classcial SRCR..., SICC..., \*\*DC..., RCA BVCC...

- Korean issues; DG RG..., Decca RD... Philips RP... (LP), JC KJCL... (LP) / KJCK... (Tape)

- LP; Columbia ML series are monaural.

- I tried to include recently available CDs first in this page, but I excluded most of all items from such labels as Iron Needle, Arkadia, Enterprise, etc. If you'd like to know the reason, see [Hall of Shame](http://www.musicandarts.com/shame.html).

- Of course also thanks so much to the owner of [mikrokosmos.com](http://www.mikrokosmos.com/) who offered me the hosting space.

- Complete list can be seen as an appendix to Serkin biography 'A life' by Stephen Lehman and Mario Faber, Oxford University Press. (Of course I cannot use it here because of the copyright)
