# Friedrich Gulda Discography

## Notes

- The source describes the discography as imperfect because Amadeo and Columbia-label recordings are incompletely documented.
- Additional information was explicitly welcomed, including recording data not on the page, broadcast recordings, and rare CDs.
- Most jazz recordings were omitted.
- LP and CD prefix explanations were delegated to the original site's label-notes page.
- Items from labels such as Iron Needle and Enterprise were excluded, with the original page linking to Music & Arts' "Hall of Shame" as the reason.
- Special thanks were given to KATO Yukihiro, Justin Urcis, late CHUN Sihyun, and M. Miguel Montfort.
